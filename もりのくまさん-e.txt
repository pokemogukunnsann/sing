One day in the forest, I met a bear.
On the path through the flower-blossoming forest, I met a bear.

The bear said,
Lady, run away.
Stomp, stomp, stomp.
Stomp, stomp, stomp.

But the bear
follows me.
Totally, totally, totally.
Totally, totally, totally.

Lady, wait a minute.
I lost something.
A small earring made of a white shell.

Oh, thank you, Mr. Bear.
Let's sing a song to thank you.
La-la-la, la-la-lalala.
La-la-la, la-la-lalala.
La-la-la, la-la-lalala.
La-la-la, la-la-lalala.
